Last updated: October 29, 2026

This site, aiforbusinesssuccessbook.com, is run by Michael Sarro ("we," "us") to offer a free white paper related to the book AI for Business Success. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

What we collect

When you sign up for the free white paper, you give us your email address and your first and last name. That is all we ask for.

Our email provider may also record basic technical information when you sign up or open our emails, such as your IP address, browser type, and whether you opened or clicked a link. This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

How we use it

  • To send you the white paper you asked for.
  • To send you occasional emails about the book, the white paper, and related work by the author, including MettaTrackAI.
  • To respond if you contact us.

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with anyone except the service providers described below.

Who handles your information

We use Systeme.io to collect sign-ups and send our emails. Systeme.io stores your email address and sign-up details on our behalf and may process them in countries outside your own. Its handling of your information is covered by its own privacy policy. Our website is hosted by Netlify, which may receive standard technical data (such as your IP address) when you visit the site.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If you unsubscribe, we may keep a record of that so we don't email you again.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email.
  • Ask us to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us at the address below. Residents of California, the European Economic Area, and the United Kingdom may have additional rights under their local laws, and we will honor valid requests.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure.
